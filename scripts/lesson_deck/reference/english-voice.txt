Let us begin slowly. Take a breath, and listen before you try to read anything. Thai is not a difficult language, but it is an unfamiliar one, and the first few sounds will feel strange in your mouth. That is normal. Say each word out loud, even when you are sure you have it wrong, because a mistake you have spoken is much easier to correct than a guess you kept quiet. We will go one small step at a time, and I will wait for you.
