## exposition three-hums
heading: Three more letters you can hum
- ง, ย and ว join ม and น, and all five share one property: you can hold the sound and stretch it without ever stopping the air.
- ngaaw nguu opens with the ng you already make at the end of "sing" — Thai simply lets that sound start a syllable instead of finishing one.
- yaaw yák glides like the y in "yes". waaw wǎaen rounds the lips the way an English w does, and at the tail of a syllable it melts away into an o.

## exposition shapes
heading: Telling the three apart
- ง is one round head with the body swerving away to the right and nothing else hung on it.
- ย carries two bumps on the same flank — no other letter you have met stacks a pair like that.
- ว closes into a single clean ring and stops there.

## retrieval which-hums
reveal: which-hums-answer
prompt: Of the three new letters, one closes into a single ring and one carries two bumps on the same flank. Which is which?

## reveal which-hums-answer
retrieval: which-hums
- ว is the single ring.
- ย is the pair of bumps.

## exposition live-syllables
heading: Live endings, and why you were taught the hums first
- Exactly five letters can close a syllable and leave it still ringing: ม, น, ง, ย and ว — the same five you can hum, which is not a coincidence.
- A syllable that ends on a long vowel with nothing after it is live too. มา is live, and so is นา.
- Anything else stops the air, and that is the other kind of ending. It arrives next lesson.

## exposition first-tone-rule
heading: Your first tone rule, applied
- All five hummed letters are low class, so every live syllable one of them opens lands flat, halfway up your speaking range.
- งาน (ngaan) is a job or a piece of work. วาง (waang) is to put something down. Both open on a hum, both end live, both sit flat.
- ยาว (yaao) is long. นาง (naang) is the title you would read as Mrs. นาย (naai) is the one you would read as Mr.

## retrieval tone-of-yaa
reveal: tone-of-yaa-answer
prompt: ยา (yaa) is medicine. Its first letter hums and its long vowel is left open with nothing after it. Which tone does the syllable take, and which rule told you so?

## reveal tone-of-yaa-answer
retrieval: tone-of-yaa
- Mid tone — flat from start to finish.
- ย is low class and the syllable is live, so the low class live rule decides it.

## exposition read-back
heading: Four words, no new letters
- งาม (ngaam) is beautiful. Read it aloud: a hum, a long vowel, a hum to close it.
- วง (wong) is a circle or a ring — a word whose shape and meaning agree for once.
- Every word on this slide is built from five letters and one vowel. That is what the sequencing buys you.
