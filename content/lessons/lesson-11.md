# The empty basin, which is silent until it is a vowel

lesson: lesson-11

<!--
Middle band, lesson 6 of 6. Declares อ as a consonant, อ as a vowel, and เ-าะ,
and closes the four-way tone grid for the mid class.
previews: none
ranks: 1-2000

อ has been on the page since lesson six, as the prop สระ อือ needs when a

เ-าะ IS THE SYMBOL THE SUITE CANNOT PROTECT. `declaredSymbols` decomposes it

never uses it" assertion passes on any deck containing those three characters

and it carries a reading challenge, an answer slide and a corner of the tone

close says the spelling settles it and names the mark as what comes for it,

## exposition aang-story
image: images/lesson-11/aang-story.jpg

heading: The woman who sold nothing
teaches: o-ang
narration: en The market has one rule about pitches and everybody obeys it. A stall keeps its place only while something of yours is standing in it. Leave it bare for a morning and by noon somebody else has moved in, and there is nothing to be done about it. So the fish-seller's sister had to go north for a whole season. On the first morning the fish-seller went down at dawn and set an empty basin in the middle of her sister's pitch. Not to sell out of. Not to wash in. ## exposition job-one
image: images/lesson-11/job-one.jpg
scene: A wide empty basin standing alone at the front of an unopened market stall with the shutters still down behind it, early morning light.
heading: Job one, and it is the basin's job
teaches: o-ang
narration: en A Thai syllable is built consonant first and vowel after. That has been true since your first lesson, and Thai holds to it everywhere. But plenty of words begin with a vowel sound and no consonant at all ahead of it. The writing system still wants a consonant in that slot. So the basin goes in. It stands at the front, it makes no sound whatsoever, and the vowel behind it is what you actually say. Exactly what the fish-seller's basin was doing on her sister's pitch, and for exactly the same reason.
- A syllable is written consonant first, and Thai keeps to that.
- Some words open on a vowel and have no consonant to write.
- The basin stands in the slot and makes no sound.

## reveal which-job-answer
retrieval: which-job
teaches: o-ang, sara-aaw
narration: en The one at the front is silent, minding an empty consonant slot for a word that opens on a vowel. The one behind a consonant is the long aw. Most of the ambiguities in this writing system want context from the words around them, and this one only wants you to look at where the mark is sitting.
